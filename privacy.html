Privacy Policy — StorePOS

Version 1 · Effective 1 October 2026 · Updated together with Terms of Service v1

StorePOS is a point-of-sale and shop-record system for small shops in Nepal. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal data we hold, why we hold it, and what your rights are. Short version: your shop's records belong to you, we never sell data, and we collect nothing beyond what the app needs to work.

1. Data we store and why

WhatWhy we need it

Shop profile (shop name, owner email, phone, address, PAN/VAT number)Your shop identity, bills, support contact, and subscription management.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, no third-party cookies — the app and website contain zero tracking technology. There is nothing to consent to, which is why you see no cookie banner.
  • No location tracking, no contact-list access, no background data collection of any kind.
  • Sign-in sessions live on your own device only (secure storage in the app, browser storage on the website).

3. Your customers' data (important)

When you type a customer's name or phone number into khata, you are responsible for that data — for example, only recording customers who do business with you, and deleting their details if they ask you to (delete the party in the app and it deletes from our servers on the next sync). We process it strictly as your storage provider and never contact your customers ourselves. WhatsApp reminders go out from your phone, under your responsibility.

4. Who else touches your data (processors)

We run on established providers, each holding only what its job needs:

  • Clerk — sign-in and identity (USA/EU).
  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting for your records.
  • Cloudinary — photo storage for products and bills.
  • Render — backend server hosting.
  • Expo / EAS — app builds and updates.
  • GitHub — source code (never your shop data).
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — only if and when you tap a reminder or support link; nothing is sent there automatically.

We disclose data to authorities only when required by Nepali law.

5. How long we keep data

  • Shop records: until you delete them (deletes sync to our servers), or until you ask us to erase everything — see §7.
  • Server logs (IP addresses, request metadata): kept short-term for operations and then discarded (hosting-provider retention, roughly 30 days).
  • Expired or cancelled shops: records are preserved (never auto-deleted), so renewing restores everything.

6. Security

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) everywhere; passwords stored as one-way bcrypt hashes, never in readable form.
  • Every shop's data is strictly isolated — accounts can only ever read or write their own shop.
  • Login secrets and passwords are stripped from server logs before storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will inform affected shop owners directly and without undue delay.

7. Your rights (access, correction, deletion)

  • See / fix: everything the app holds about you is visible and editable in the app itself (Profile, Settings, records).
  • Delete everything: message your seller (WhatsApp 9742906882) asking for full erasure — we remove your shop and records from our servers. Copies on your own phones remain yours to delete (uninstall the app).
  • Export: ask and we will provide your shop's records in a portable format (CSV).

8. Children and business use

StorePOS is a business tool for shop owners aged 18 and above. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly hold children's data beyond what a shop owner enters in the course of business (covered by §3).

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes get a new version number and an in-app prompt before continuing — the same mechanism as the Terms of Service. Minor wording fixes do not trigger a re-prompt.

10. Law and contact

These terms are governed by the laws of Nepal. Privacy questions or requests: contact your seller directly — WhatsApp 9742906882.
